About

            
Here's a little about me..

          

          

            

              I'm ujji_k06, a Roblox developer who works across
              the whole Luau stack. I'm not a one-trick scripter — I build
              gameplay loops, data and saves, economies, UI, secure networking,
              and the frameworks that keep all of it clean as a project grows.
            

            

              I've been building on Roblox since 2022. Most of
              my public work is technical demos and reusable frameworks, and I
              am actively looking to be part of a team so I can grow my own
              portfolio. If your game needs a reliable scripter who won't ghost
              you, that's exactly me.
